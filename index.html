Let's Cook!
9 easy steps · ~25 minutes
1
🧱Wake Up the Soya!
Boil 800ml of water with a pinch of salt. Add the soya chunks and let them bubble away for 8 minutes.

Drain them, rinse with cold water, then squeeze each chunk firmly — like you're wringing out a tiny sponge! This removes the raw taste. Chop them in half.
⏱️ This takes 10 minutes
2
🌾Rinse the Rice!
Wash the rice in 2–3 changes of cold water until the water runs clear and clean. Soak it for 10 minutes, then drain before using.
💡 Do this while soya is boiling!
3
🥕Chop the Veggie Gang!
• Slice onion thin
• Dice carrots into small cubes (~1cm)
• Chop beans into 1cm pieces
• Dice capsicum into 2cm chunks
• Roughly chop tomatoes
• Slit the green chillies lengthwise
• Wash and roughly chop mint & coriander
⚡ Do this in parallel with Step 1!
4
🔥The Sizzle Moment!
Heat ghee + oil together in your pressure cooker on medium heat. Add the whole spices — cumin seeds, bay leaves, cardamom, cloves, cinnamon. Sauté for 30 seconds until your kitchen smells AMAZING!
⚠️ Don't burn them — just golden and fragrant!
5
🧅The Flavour Base!
Add onion slices. Stir around for 2 minutes until soft and translucent. Add ginger-garlic paste + green chillies. Stir for another 30 seconds.

Don't brown the onion — just make it soft and sweet!
6
🍅Veggies Join the Party!
Add tomatoes and cook until pulpy (2 min). Then toss in carrots, beans, peas, and capsicum — stir for 1 minute. Now add all the ground spices: turmeric, chilli powder, coriander powder, garam masala, and salt. Give it a good stir for 30 seconds!
7
🧱The Main Team Arrives!
Add the squeezed soya chunks — mix gently. Add the drained rice — fold gently (don't break those grains!). Stir in mint and coriander.

Now, place paneer cubes on top — DO NOT MIX! Just lay them gently on top like a cozy blanket.
🧀 Paneer on top = stays soft and perfect!
8
🫧The Pressure Magic!
Pour 300ml water gently around the edges (not over the paneer). Close the cooker. Cook for 3 whistle on medium heat, then switch off!

Now wait patiently for 10 minutes of natural pressure release. Don't force it open!
🧘 This rest time = perfect fluffy rice!
9
🍋The Grand Finale!
Open carefully. Squeeze ½ lemon over the top. Gently fluff with a fork — don't mash! Scatter fresh coriander on top.
🎉 Ready to eat! Divide into 4 portions!

Secret Tips!
for perfect results every time
🤜
Squeeze soya hard! Wet soya = bland and raw-tasting. Squeeze every chunk firmly.
💧
Only 300ml water! Soya holds lots of water, so less is more here.
🧘
Wait for natural release! Force-opening toughens the paneer. Be patient!
🧀
Paneer on top, don't mix! Laying it on top keeps cubes beautifully intact.
⚖️
Weigh ingredients the first time. After that, you'll know by eye!
📦
Portion right away! Pack into 4 containers immediately for easy grab-and-go meals.
