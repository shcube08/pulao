# 🍲 Soya Pulao — High-Protein Vegetarian One-Pot Meal

> **59g protein per serving · 25 minutes · One pot · Batch-cook friendly**

A complete, restaurant-quality Indian meal built around soya chunks and paneer. Delivers roughly **3× the protein of a typical Indian meal** at similar or lower calories — all from whole, unprocessed ingredients.

| Ingredient | Quantity |

## 👨‍🍳 Method

### Step 1 — Prep the soya (10 min)
1. Boil 800ml water with ½ tsp salt
2. Add soya chunks, boil for **8 minutes** on medium heat
3. Drain, rinse with cold water
4. **Squeeze each chunk firmly** to remove excess water and raw bean taste — this step matters
5. Chop chunks in half for better texture

### Step 2 — Prep the rice (3 min)
1. Rinse in 2–3 changes of cold water until water runs clear
2. Soak for 10 min while prepping other things
3. Drain before using

### Step 3 — Chop vegetables (8 min — do in parallel with Step 1)
Slice onion thin · Dice carrots to ~1cm · Chop beans to 1cm · Dice capsicum to 2cm · Chop tomatoes · Slit chillies · Chop mint and coriander

### Step 4 — Tempering (1 min)
Heat ghee + oil in the pressure cooker on medium. Add whole spices. Sauté **30 seconds** until fragrant — don't burn.

### Step 5 — Aromatics (3 min)
Add onion, soften for 2 min (don't brown). Add ginger-garlic paste + chillies, stir 30 seconds.

### Step 6 — Build the masala (4 min)
Add tomatoes, cook until pulpy (~2 min). Add all remaining vegetables, stir 1 min. Add all ground spices + salt, stir 30 seconds.

### Step 7 — Add the main components (2 min)
1. Add squeezed soya, mix gently
2. Add drained rice, fold in gently — don't break the grains
3. Add mint and coriander, stir once
4. **Lay paneer cubes on top — do not mix.** This keeps them intact.

### Step 8 — Pressure cook (15 min including natural release)
1. Pour 300ml water gently around the edges (not over paneer)
2. Close lid
3. **1 whistle on medium heat**, then switch off immediately
4. **Natural pressure release for 10 minutes** — don't force open

### Step 9 — Finish (1 min)
Open carefully. Squeeze ½ lemon over the top. Fluff gently with a fork. Garnish with fresh coriander.

## 💡 Tips for Best Results

- **Squeeze soya thoroughly** — wet soya tastes raw and bland, no shortcuts here
- **Use only 300ml water** — soya releases moisture during cooking, less is more
- **Don't skip natural pressure release** — paneer toughens if force-released
- **Paneer on top, always** — mixing breaks the cubes
- **Weigh ingredients your first time** — eyeball it confidently after that
- **Portion into 4 containers immediately** — makes grab-and-go meals effortless all week

## 🌱 Why This Works Nutritionally

1. **Complete proteins** — soya is one of the few plant proteins with all essential amino acids; paneer adds BCAAs
2. **Slow-release carbs** — rice + high vegetable volume = gradual glucose release
3. **High fiber** — 14.4g per serve supports gut health and satiety
4. **Balanced fats** — ghee (heat-stable saturated) + groundnut oil (MUFA) at a minimal 15g total
5. **Wide micronutrient coverage** — calcium from paneer, iron from soya, Vitamin A & C from vegetables, B-vitamins throughout
6. **No added sugar, no preservatives, no ultra-processed ingredients**

Suitable for **cutting, recomping, or maintenance** — adjust portion size to your caloric needs.
